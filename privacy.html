Privacy Policy

Snapfuse · Last updated: October 16, 2026

In short: Snapfuse does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. Your photos and designs stay on your device.

Who we are

Snapfuse is developed by Dwaraha T ("we", "us"). Contact: Dwaraha.rpht@outlook.com.

Information we collect

None. Snapfuse has no user accounts, no servers that receive your data, no advertising, and no third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools. The app does not make network requests with your content.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following in its own private storage on your device. It is never sent to us:

  • Your saved designs (projects) and their photos, so you can reopen and edit them.
  • Exported images shown under Projects → Exports.

This data is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall Snapfuse.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
Photos (add only)Used only to save your finished collages, cards and stories to your photo library. The app cannot read your whole library.
Photo pickerYou choose photos with Apple's system picker. Snapfuse only receives the photos you select; no permission is needed.
CameraUsed only in the Measure tool if you choose to take a photo of an item to estimate its size. Photos are processed on your device.

On-device processing

Face detection, passport photo fitting, background removal and item measurement use Apple's Vision framework and run entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Sharing

If you tap Share, your design is passed to the app you pick (Messages, Mail, etc.) through the iOS share sheet. That app's privacy policy then applies. We do not receive a copy.

Children

Snapfuse does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID / Touch ID and iOS encryption).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email Dwaraha.rpht@outlook.com.
