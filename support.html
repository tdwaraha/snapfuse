Frequently asked questions

Where are my designs saved?

Designs autosave inside the app and appear under Projects → Designs. Exported images appear under Projects → Exports. Everything is stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this data, so save finished work to Photos first.

How do I save or share my finished design?

Open the design, tap Export, then choose Save to Photos or Share. Saving to Photos asks for "add photos" permission the first time.

Snapfuse can't save to my photo library.

Go to Settings → Snapfuse → Photos and allow adding photos. Then try exporting again.

The camera doesn't work in Measure.

The camera is only used in the Measure tool. Enable it in Settings → Snapfuse → Camera. You can also choose a photo from your library instead.

Are passport photo sizes official?

Sizes follow published government guidance at the time of release, but requirements change. Always confirm the latest rules on the official government website before submitting or printing. Snapfuse does not guarantee acceptance of any photo.

How do I print a design at the right size?

Choose a product in Print (or a custom size), design on the canvas, then export a print-ready PDF at true size. For sublimation, turn on Mirror. Print at 100% / "Actual size" with no scaling.

Does Snapfuse upload my photos?

No. Face detection and background removal run on your device using Apple's Vision framework. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual designs and exports inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. We hold no copy because nothing is sent to us.
