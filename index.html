Snapfuse

Collage & card maker for photos, birthdays, stories, passports and print — all on your iPhone.

What you can make

Collages & cards

17 grid layouts, 24 shape layouts and 11 frame layouts for up to 16 photos.

~550 templates

Birthdays, holidays, travel, weddings, pets, quotes, business and more.

Full design editor

Text, stickers, shapes, frames, filters, layers, multiple pages, undo and redo.

Passport & ID photos

Head-size guides, on-device face fit, white background and print sheets for many countries.

Print & sublimation

Mugs, magnets, coasters, keychains and more at 300 dpi with bleed, cut guides and mirrored PDFs.

Measure from a photo

Use a bank card or paper beside an item to estimate its size and pick the nearest blank.

Private by design

Snapfuse works on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no uploads. Your photos stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later.
